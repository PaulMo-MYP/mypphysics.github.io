The AC Generator

A coil turns in the field between two stator magnets. Slip rings and carbon brushes take the induced emf out to a centre-zero voltmeter. The graph plots that output voltage against the angle of the coil.

Revolutions per second (frequency f)
Magnetic flux density B
Coil area fixed at A = 10 cm × 10 cm = 0.010 m²
Coil angle θ
Output voltage V
Peak voltage V0 = NBAω
Angular speed ω
Period T
V = NBAω sin θ    where  ω = 2πf
θ = 0° when the coil plane is perpendicular to the field. The flux linkage is then a maximum, but it is not changing, so V = 0. The output peaks at 90° and 270°, when the coil sides cut the field lines fastest.
